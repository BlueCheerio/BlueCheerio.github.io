About Me

My passion for electrical engineering started in high school during a welding class amidst the COVID-19 pandemic. While studying the physical, electrical, and material science driving arc welding, I was fascinated by how fundamental electromagnetic forces work together to shape physical structures. That revelation instantly made me realize I wanted to pursue electrical engineering.

Today, I am particularly interested in electrical construction engineering as well as optical technology—specifically the intricate circuitry, hardware controls, and image sensor engineering behind modern digital cameras.
